## 📌 About Sunforge

Sunforge provides practical digital tools and business administration support tailored for small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs), service businesses, consultants, and growing teams. Remote-first, operating out of Abuja, Nigeria.

---

## 🚀 Services Offered

* **Business Websites**: Fast, mobile-friendly websites that clearly communicate services and capture customer leads.
* **Workflow & Spreadsheet Trackers**: Custom Google Sheets and Excel dashboards to turn messy paperwork and workloads into structured, readable tracking systems.
* **Document & Brand Formatting**: Professional document layout editing, letterhead styling, digital signature integration (for emails and official documents), and custom logo creation.
* **AI Prompting & Tech Coaching**: 1-on-1 and team consultation teaching practical AI prompting (ChatGPT, Gemini) and core Microsoft Office / Google Suite tools to speed up daily work.
* **Digital Record Systems**: Structured, searchable record registers for inventory, job logs, and staff management.
* **Website Care & Support**: Ongoing site updates, maintenance checks, and technical assistance.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools

* **Frontend**: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (Vanilla ES6+)
* **Form Handling**: Web3Forms integration
* **Typography**: DM Sans & Manrope (Google Fonts)
* **Design & Branding**: Custom graphics, CSS Grid/Flexbox layouts
